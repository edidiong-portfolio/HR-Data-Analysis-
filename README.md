### Project Overview

The dashboard combines **employee, lifecycle, salary, and awards data** to help HR understand workforce trends, compensation, recognition, and retention risks.

---

### Tools Used

* **Power BI** – Dashboard creation and visualization
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – KPI and measure calculations

##  Key Insights

- Workforce gender representation is relatively balanced.
- Most employees are aged **40–59**.
- Operations receives the most awards, while Sales receives fewer.
- Many employee exits are linked to **retirement and contract endings**.
- Recognition levels vary across employee groups.

---

###  Recommendations

1. Develop younger employees for future leadership.
2. Review recognition fairness across employee groups.
3. Increase recognition in low-award departments.
4. Expand internship and graduate hiring.
5. Use employee feedback to reduce voluntary turnover.
